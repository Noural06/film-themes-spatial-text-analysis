# Place, Perception, and Narrative in Film Data

## Spatial and textual analysis of violence and poverty themes

An MSc Data Science group project investigating whether violence-related and
poverty-related themes are more prevalent in films produced in the Southern
Hemisphere than in the Northern Hemisphere between 1990 and 2024.

## Research approach

- Source: IMDb film summaries and country-of-origin information
- Text analysis: transformer embeddings and semantic similarity
- Geographic analysis: country mapping, hemisphere classification and spatial
  aggregation
- Study period: 1990-2024
- Themes: violence and poverty

## Main findings

- No clear geographic clustering was identified.
- Only small thematic differences were observed between hemispheres.
- Violence-related and poverty-related signals tended to co-occur in film
  narratives.
- Country-level variation existed, but narrative themes were not strongly
  geographically determined.

## Limitations

- Imbalanced film coverage across countries and hemispheres
- No ground-truth labels for the narrative themes
- Results were sensitive to semantic-similarity thresholds
- Geographic origin does not fully capture the cultural context of a film
